# Privacy Policy for Dnyany

_Last updated: November 1, 2026_

Dnyany ("the app") is a math practice app. This policy explains what
information the app collects and how it is used.

## Information We Collect

Dnyany does not require you to create an account, and we do not
collect your name, email address, or any other contact details
through the app.

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to
manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription
status information and an anonymous app user identifier so we can
unlock access after you subscribe, restore purchases and see whether
your subscription is active. We do not sell this information and do
not use it for advertising. Learn more at
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## How Your Data Is Stored

Your progress, scores, streaks, and settings are stored only on your
own device, using standard local app storage. This information never
leaves your device and is never sent to us or to any third party.

If you delete the app, this locally stored data is deleted along
with it.

## Advertising and Analytics

Dnyany does not display advertisements and does not use advertising
or tracking services. The only service that receives data from the
app is RevenueCat, described above, which manages subscriptions and
provides us with aggregate subscription statistics. We do not use any
other analytics service, and we do not use this information to track
you across apps or websites.

## Children's Privacy

Dnyany does not ask for personal information and has no accounts,
advertising, or tracking. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. The only data that leaves the device is
the anonymous subscription information handled by RevenueCat and the
app store, as described above.

Parents or guardians who have questions, or who want information
removed, can contact us at the email address below.

## Changes to This Policy

If Dnyany's data practices change in the future (for example, if
online or social features are added), this policy will be updated
to reflect those changes before they take effect.
