# Privacy Policy for Dnyany

_Last updated: September 19, 2026_

Dnyany ("the app") is a math practice app. This policy explains what
information the app collects and how it is used.

## Information We Collect

Dnyany does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

The app does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide
your name, email address, or any other personal details.

## How Your Data Is Stored

Your progress, scores, streaks, and settings are stored only on your
own device, using standard local app storage. This information never
leaves your device and is never sent to us or to any third party.

If you delete the app, this locally stored data is deleted along
with it.

## Advertising and Analytics

Dnyany does not display advertisements and does not use any
third-party analytics or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

Dnyany is designed to be safe for use by children. Because the app
collects no personal information from anyone, including children
under 13, it does not knowingly collect personal information from
children in a way that would require parental consent under COPPA
or similar laws.

## Changes to This Policy

If Dnyany's data practices change in the future (for example, if
online or social features are added), this policy will be updated
to reflect those changes before they take effect.
